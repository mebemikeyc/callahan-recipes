### Ingredients

#### Brine
- 3 cups apple cider (or apple juice)
- 2 gallons cold water
- 4 tablespoons fresh rosemary leaves
- 5 cloves of garlic minced
- 1 1/2 cups kosher salt
- 2 cups light brown sugar
- 3 tablespoons whole black peppercorns
- 5 whole bay leaves
- Peels of 3 oranges

#### Dry Rub
I made a double batch to make sure I had enough.
- 1/4 cup salt
- 1/4 cup turbinado sugar
- 1 tsp granulated garlic
- 1 tsp granulated onion
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp thyme
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp mustard
- 1 tsp oregano

### Instructions

#### Turkey Preparation

I like to spatchcock the turkey when I smoke it.  I like to have some extra bones to make stock with, since the drippings are lost when smoking.

1. Cut out the spine of the turkey.
1. Put the turkey on a cutting board and press flat to break the breast bone.
1. I also cut off the wing tips to save for stock.

#### Brine
1. Combine all ingredients in a large pot and bring to a boil, stirring to dissolve salt and sugar.
1. Turn off the heat, cover and allow the brine to cool completely.
1. Put a brine bag in a clean 5 gallon bucket.  Put the turkey in that bag.
1. Pour the brine over the turkey to cover, and refrigerate (or in a cooler covered with ice) until ready to cook.

#### Dry Rub
1. Put all ingredients in a mixing bowl and stir until well combined.

#### Preparation
1. Remove the turkey from the brine and rinse thoroughly.
1. Season the turkey all over with the dry rub.
1. Put the turkey in the smoker, positioned with the breast away from the heat source, cooking "hot and fast".  I think most of the time, it was close to 350º F.
1. When the breast reaches 155º F, remove the turkey and place in a clean cooler for an hour or so to rest.
