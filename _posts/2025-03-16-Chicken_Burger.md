Burgers
=======

### Ingredients

* 2 pounds ground chicken
* 1 red pepper, one onion, processed in food processor, strained to remove excess liquid
* \~1/2 bunch of cilantro and 2 cloves of garlic, food processed
* 1/2 cup panko
* 1 tsp cumin
* 1 tsp smoked paprika
* 1/2 tsp garlic powder
* 1/2 tsp onion powder
* 1/2 tsp coriander
* 1 tsp salt
* 1 tsp pepper
* 1/4 tsp cayenne

### Instructions

1. Food process red pepper and onion and drain excess liquid in mesh strainer.
2. Food process the cilantro and garlic
3. Combine all ingredients until well blended
4. Form into patties and cook

  

Southwestern Mayo
=================

This was a little spicy for Crystal.  Next time I might use 1/2 chipotle powder, 1/2 smoked paprika.

#### Ingredients

* about 1/2 bunch cilantro
* 1 cup mayo
* 4 tsp chipotle powder
* 1 tsp ground mustard seed
* 1 tsp garlic powder
* 1/2 tsp salt
* 1 tsp brown sugar
* Juice from 1/2 lime

#### Instructions

1. Food process cilantro until finely chopped
2. Combine all ingredients in food processor, pulse until well mixed.
