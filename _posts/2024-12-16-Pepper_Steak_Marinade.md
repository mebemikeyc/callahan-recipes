This is my attempt at McKinnon's "cannonball" marinade.  I remember it being having a strong peppers and onions flavor with a touch of heat.  I'm not sure it actually represents the original marinade at all anymore, but I like how it comes out.


## Ingredients
- 2 Red peppers, sliced
- 1 green pepper, sliced
- Handful of garlic cloves, crushed
- 3 onions, sliced
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes
- Olive oil for frying
- Some salt
- Some pepper.  Maybe 1 tbsp?  Depends how much pepper flavor you want
- 1/3 cup soy sauce
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup red wine vinegar
- 1 tablespoon MSG

  
## Original Instructions
1. Put peppers, onions, olive oil, garlic, salt and pepper in a pot.  Start on high to fry for a bit, then lower to simmer, covered for 1/2 hour.
1. Add sugar, soy sauce, red wine vinegar, and blend to make smooth.  I used an immersion blender.
1. Chill for awhile, then pour over steak tips.

## Modified Instructions
1. Food process the peppers, garlic and onion together
1. Mix all the other ingredients together in a bowl.
1. Add the steak tips and let them soak for awhile.

This resulted in very tasty steak tips, but they weren't as tender as I'd hoped.  Also, the marinade didn't permeate as well as it could.  I think some mechanical tenderizing and vacuum marinating will help a lot.
