Who doesn't love a nice, somewhat spicy, Buffalo chicken sandwich?!  Though I've tried many times to perfect it, this is the closest I've come.  It starts with pickle brined boneless chicken thighs, breaded with panko and regular plain bread crumbs, loaded up with plenty of thinly sliced pickles and buttermilk ranch celery "slaw", on a nice bulkie roll.

## Ingredients:
- 6 boneless skinless chicken thighs
- All purpose flour (for dredging)
- A couple eggs and milk for egg wash
- Panko breadcrumbs
- Plain breadcrumbs
- A jar of Mount Olive dill picles
- 6 bulkie rolls
- 1 bunch of celery
- Hidden Valley Buttermilk Ranch Dressing
- 1 cup Frank's red hot sauce
- 1 stick of cold butter
- A tablespoon or two of Dijon mustard

## Instructions: 

### Chicken

#### Brine
1. Trim any stray cartilage or excessive fat from the chicken thighs, being sure to keep them mostly intact.
1. Put them in a bowl and pour pickle juice over them until mostly submerged.
1. Let them brine for a few hours, flipping periodically to ensure even brine distribution.

#### Breading
I like to bread the chicken beforehand and let them chill in the fridge on a cooling rack on a sheet tray. That way the starches can absorb some moisture, and less stuff falls off in the oil.

1. Take the chicken out of the pickle juice, and dredge thoroughly in flour.
1. Dip the dredged chicken in the egg wash, then roll in the breadcrumbs.
1. Put on a rack on a sheet tray and let them rest for awhile in the fridge.


#### Frying

My usual go-to is frying at 325ºF.  Last time, the breading browned long before the chicken was cooked through.  Probably lower temperature would work better.

1. Heat a pan with vegetable oil to ~325º.
1. Fry until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165ºF.


### Toppings
1. Thinly slice pickles into circles.  Thinly slice celery on a bias to break down the fiber more.  I use a mandolin on the thinnest setting to get them nice and thin, and consistent.
1. Mix the celery with the buttermilk ranch dressing.  Add enough that they're suitably saturated, but not swimming.
1. I put both in deli containers for easy distribution when assembling.  Refrigerate until ready

### Buffalo Sauce
1. In a wide, shallow pan, heat up 1 cup of Frank's Red Hot sauce.  Get it hot, but not boiling.
1. Whisk in some dijon mustard.  Maybe a tablespoon or two.  This acts as an emulsifier to keep the butter from separating.
1. Take the pan off the heat.
1. Cut the butter into small pieces and melt into the hot sauce.  Whisk until melted and well combined.

### Assembly
1. Put a solid handful of sliced pickles on the bottom half of the bulkie roll.
1. Coat a piece of chicken in the Buffalo sauce and put on top of the sliced pickles.
1. Put a good covering of ranch celery slaw on top of the chicken.
1. Top with the other half of the bulkie roll.
