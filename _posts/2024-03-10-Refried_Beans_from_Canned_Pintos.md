I made these simple refried beans from canned beans.  They were so surprisingly tasty, I had to write it down before I forgot.  

### Ingredients
- 2 cans pinto beans, drained and rinsed
- A few tablespoons of fat. I used vegetable oil, but lard is probably better
- 1/2 onion, finely chopped
- A few cloves of garlic, chopped.  I think I used 4
- 1/2 cup water. More as needed
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 lime, juiced

### Instructions
1. Add the oil to a pot and put on medium high heat (one you can use metal stuff in)
1. Cook onions and garlic until softened.
1. Add chili powder and cumin and cook until fragrant.
1. Add beans and water. Turn down heat and cook covered for 5 minutes.
1. Mash with potato masher until desired consistency is reached.  Add more water if needed.  Cook 1onger if needed.  I didn't have to.
1. Add juice of 1/2 lime and mix in.
1. Season with salt as needed
