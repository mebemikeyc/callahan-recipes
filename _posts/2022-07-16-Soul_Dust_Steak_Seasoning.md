So, we had two seasonings from our yearly trip to Saint Simon's Island, Georgia.  One was "Southern Soul" BBQ's "Soul Dust".  The other was a "3 beer" chipotle / cayenne rub that didn't have much flavor other than smoky and a little spicy.  This is my attempt at recreating it.  
Ingredients:* 1/2 cup kosher salt
* 1/2 cup turbinado sugar
* 2/3 cup light brown sugar
* 1 1/2 tablespoon garlic powder
* 1 1/2 tablespoon onion powder
* 3 tablespoons paprika (might use smoked next time)
* 1 tablespoons freshly ground pepper
* 2 tbsp Lawry's lemon pepper seasoning
* 1/2 tbsp chili powder
* 1 tablespoon dried thyme
* 1 1/2 tablespoons chipotle
* 1/2 tablespoon of cayenne
* 1 1/2 tablespoon ground cumin
