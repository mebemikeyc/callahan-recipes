Posted as "Michael's clam chowder".  Apparently it once won the Hampton Beach chowder festival

  

3 cans of chopped clams in clam juice and one of those containers of clams from the seafood counter

2 big jars of clam juice

A pound of bacon chopped then fried

A whole stick of sweet cream butter! 😳

3 or 4 stalks of celery chopped

A large vidalia onion chopped

A half of a bag of baby white potatoes cut in quarters

3\-5 garlic cloves minced

A cup or a cup and a half of flour depending on how thick you want it

Bay leaves, parsley, oregano, salt, thyme and pepper the dried ones

A half of a quart of heavy cream 🤦‍♀️

This is what sounded weird to me.

Cook the chopped bacon up and drain it, leaving the bacon fat in the pan.

Then you add a whole stick of butter to the bacon fat then add the onion, celery and garlic. Simmer that for a few minutes. Then add the potatoes, add the flour and make a roux mixing with a whisk then add all the clam juice and seasonings and let that simmer for a half hour on med low or until the potatoes are soft. Stir it a lot. Then add all the clams and heavy cream and let it cook in low for an hour. Adding pepper if you like it. He never added salt to the chowder.
