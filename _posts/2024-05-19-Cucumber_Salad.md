I love this stuff with BBQ.  It's kinda like a pickle salad, and comes together real easy.  I always make at least a double batch.

For scaling, 2 lbs of cucumbers is roughly 6 cups, so triple batch.   Scale all ingredients x 3.

I typically make a double batch.  Each package of mini cucumbers turns out to be roughly 2 cups.

## Ingredients:
- 2 cups thinly-sliced (pickling) cucumbers
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup vinegar, preferably good apple cider vinegar
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp dill weed
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 dash cayenne pepper

## Instructions:
1. Thinly slice the cucumbers and red onions with a mandolin, and put in a bowl.
1. Add all other ingredients to a pot and bring just to a boil.  Stir to dissolve / incorporate the mix.
1. Pour over the cucumbers and onions.  Stir to distribute.
1. Let the mix soften on the counter for awhile.  Stir every once and awhile to to evenly pickle.
1. When the cucumbers and onions are softened, move the mix to the refrigerator.
