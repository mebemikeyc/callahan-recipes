This is Alex's copycat recipe from Corner Butcher's Olive marinade (named after their dog).  I had never thought of using olives or olive oil in a marinade.  Definitely need to try it!Ingredients:

* 1/2 cup green olives, chopped
* 3 tablespoons olive brine
* 4 tablespoons olive oil
* 4 cloves of garlic
* 1 tablespoon lemon juice
* 1 teaspoon dried oregano
* 1 teaspoon paprika
* 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
* 1/2 teaspoon salt
* 1/4 teaspoon chili flakes
