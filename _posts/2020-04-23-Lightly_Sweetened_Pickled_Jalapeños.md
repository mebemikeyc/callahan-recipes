9 sizeable jalapeños, sliced into nice thick slices  
2 cups plain white vinegar  
2 cups water  
4 smashed cloves of garlic  
3 tablespoons kosher salt  
1/4 cup white sugar  
  
Boil and stir all ingredients except jalapeños  
Add jalapeños, kill the heat, give a little stir  
Set aside to cool
