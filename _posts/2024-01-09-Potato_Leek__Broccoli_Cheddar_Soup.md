### Ingredients

* 1 Stick butter
* 1 medium onion, diced
* 1 leek, diced and thoroughly rinsed
* 1 broccoli crown, florets separated from stalks.  Stalks small diced (1/4 inch)
* 2 or 3 carrots, peeled and small diced
* 4 or 5 Russet / Idaho potatoes, peeled, large diced (3/4 inch)
* Around 2 quarts chicken stock / broth / bouillon and water, whatever. More or less depending on your soup
* Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
* 8 ounce stick of sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
* All purpose flour for roux.  Maybe 1/4 cup.
* Heavy cream to finish

### Instructions

1. Prepare all the ingredients.  Combine onion, leek, carrots and broccoli stalks
2. Melt butter in 4 quart pan over medium heat.  Add onion, carrots, broccoli stalks and leeks and cook until softened.
3. Add all purpose flour to thicken, and cook to get rid of floury taste.
4. Add potatoes and broccoli florets.  Add chicken broth to cover veggies.  Cook at medium/low boil until potatoes are soft.
5. Add cheddar cheese and stir to combine
6. Add black pepper as needed.  I probably added \~1 tbsp.  I like pepper.  Season to suit your taste.
7. Finish with heavy cream, just enough to be creamy.
8. Serve with crusty bread, and maybe some fresh diced scallions.
