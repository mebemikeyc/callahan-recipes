Chicken breast butterflied and pounded thin. Salt and pepper both sides

Use your favorite hamSwiss cheese (tonight I used both swiss and cheddar slices)Heat butter and garlic power in a small pan over medium, add panko crumbs until golden brownLayer ham and cheese on chicken. Roll and hold closed with tooth picks.Brush egg wash on chicken and roll in your toasted panko.Put in 350° oven for 35\-40 minutes
