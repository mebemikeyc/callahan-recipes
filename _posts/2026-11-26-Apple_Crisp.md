### Ingredients

#### Filling
- 6 apples, peeled, cored and sliced
- 2 tablespoons white sugar
- 3/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 or 2 tablespoons lemon juice

#### Topping
- 1 cup light brown sugar
- 3/4 cup old fashioned oats
- 3/4 cup all purpose flour
- 1 stick cold unsalted butter, diced into cubes
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- pinch of kosher salt


### Instructions

#### Filling
1. Peel, core and slice the apples and put in a mixing bowl
1. Toss with sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice until well incorporated.

#### Topping
1. Put all ingredients in a mixing bowl
1. Cut the butter in.  I don't have a pastry knife, so I just mix the butter in by squeezing the butter cubes and mixing with the flour to incorporate.  Repeat until everything is well homogenized.

#### Assembly
1. Grease a 9x13 Pyrex dish.  Preheat oven to 350º F.
1. Add the apple filling to the pan.
1. Sprinkle the topping on top of the apples and spread out to evenly distribute.
1. Bake for 40 - 50 minutes until bubbling, topping starts to brown, and apples are soft.  Note, it will continue to cook and soften after removing. Don't cook till it's TOO soft, or you'll have applesauce crisp.
