Started with this:[https://www.365daysofcrockpot.com/slow\-cooker\-cilantro\-lime\-shredded\-pork/](https://www.365daysofcrockpot.com/slow-cooker-cilantro-lime-shredded-pork/)  
  
* 5lbs pork sirloin roast
* 1/2 cupfresh lime juice (used juice of 6 limes)
* 2 tbspchili powder
* 2 tbspcumin
* 4 tspkosher salt
* 1 cupchopped cilantro (used 2 small bunches)
* 2 tbsp minced garlic
* 1 onion
* 1/2 small can of chipotles

Put pork in slow cookerPut all other ingredients in food processor and blend until smooth.Pour over pork and let it go.
