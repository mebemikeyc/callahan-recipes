This is my take on a po' boy that the whole family will eat.  Rather than shrimp, oyster or other fish/shellfish, I use fresh fried chicken tenders.

The key to these sandwiches is freshness.  Fresh bread, fresh made chicken tenders, fresh veggies and pickles, freshly made remoulade.  Good stuff!!

### Remoulade

Mix everything together in a bowol or blender, whatever you feel like.  Refrigerate for awhile to let the flavors meld.

- 1 1/4 cup Mayo
- 2 tablespoon Chopped pickles (fuck cornichons. Sweet pickles are the devil.)
- 1 tablespoon chopped capers
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1/4 cup Dijon Mustard / course mustard
- 1 tablespoon Louisiana Hot sauce
- 2 tsp minced garlic
- 1 Scallion finely chopped
- 2 teaspoons Slap Your Mama, Low Sodium -- Cajun seasoning
- 1/8 teaspoon cayenne
- 2 teaspoons paprika
- 1 - 2 teaspoons prepared horseradish. Optional, but adds a lot to the flavor. I used to completely leave it off.

### Chicken Fingers

#### Ingredients
- 4 Chicken breasts, filleted and pounded thin for even frying
- Flour for dredging
- 3 eggs, beaten with milk (egg wash for dredged chicken)
- Plain panko bread crumbs
- Plain regular bread crumbs
- Vegetable oil for frying


#### Instructions

1. Fillet the chicken and pound thin for even frying.
1. Dredge in flour, wet in egg wash, roll in panko/plain breadcrumb mixture.
1. Fry in vegetable oil until cooked through and crispy.
1. Cut into bite size pieces.


### Toppings

- thin sliced red onion
- sliced pickles
- banana peppers
- thin sliced tomato
- shredded lettuce?  I think I added once, but really there's not usually room.

### Assembly

1. Cut a French bread in half lengthwise.  Hollow out the soft bread.  Makes room for more toppings so you don't have to unhinge your jaw to eat.
1. Spread the remoulade on both sides of the hollowed out bread
1. Place a layer of pickles on the bottom of the bread.
1. Place bite size chicken pieces on top of the pickles, on the bottom bread.
1. Give the chicken a nice shower of Louisiana hot sauce.  Optional, but yeah, it rules.
1. Add a layer of 
1. Top with pickles and banana peppers
1. Top with tomatoes
1. Top with lettuce
1. Sprinkle more hot sauce on top of the lettuce and cap it all off.
