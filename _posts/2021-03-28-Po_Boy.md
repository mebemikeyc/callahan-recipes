This is my take on a po' boy that the whole family will eat.  Rather than shrimp, oyster or other fish/shellfish, I use fresh fried chicken tenders.

The key to these sandwiches is freshness.  Fresh bread, fresh made chicken tenders, fresh veggies and pickles, freshly made remoulade.  Good stuff!!

### Remoulade

Just mix everything together in a bowl.  Refrigerate for awhile to let the flavors meld.* 1 1/4 cup Mayo
* 2 tablespoon Chopped pickles (fuck cornichons. Sweet pickles are the devil.)
* 1 tablespoon chopped capers
* 1 tablespoon lemon juice
* 1/4 cup Dijon Mustard / course mustard
* 1 tablespoon Louisiana Hot sauce
* 2 tsp minced garlic
* 1 Scallion finely chopped
* 2 teaspoons Slap Your Mama, Low Sodium \-\- Cajun seasoning
* 1/8 teaspoon cayenne
* 2 teaspoons paprika
* 1\-2 teaspoons prepared horseradish \-\- I didn't add this, but might next time

### Chicken Fingers

Cut chicken breasts into thinner pieces.  Dredge in flour, dip in egg/milk wash, roll in mix of plain regular bread crumbs and plain panko bread crumbs.  Fry in vegetable oil until crispy.  Cut in smaller pieces.  Last time I cut the chicken into small bite size pieces.  That worked very well.

### Toppings

* thin sliced red onion
* sliced pickles
* banana peppers (sometimes)
* thin sliced tomato
* shredded lettuce

### Assembly

1. Cut a French bread in half lengthwise.  Hollow out the soft bread.
2. Spread the remoulade on both sides of the hollowed out bread
3. Cut up chicken to bite size pieces and lay out on bottom half of French bread
4. Top with hot sauce and onions
5. Top with pickles and banana peppers
6. Top with tomatoes
7. Top with lettuce
8. Sprinkle more hot sauce on top of the lettuce and cap it all off.
