While we were in Texas, we went to Franklin's BBQ.  They had a "Ruby's Dang Good Pie".  This reminded us of Crystal's sister, who makes amazing desserts... She goes by "Ruby Dragon".  Well, we forgot to order it, but did a search and discovered it was a recipe from Ruby's BBQ for "Dang Good Pie".  We made this one.  It might need some tweaking, but it was pretty good.  I felt like it could use some vanilla, and maybe some condensed milk.

## Ingredients
- 3 large eggs
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 1 cup shredded coconut (we used sweetened)
- 3 tbsp all purpose flour
- 1 cup crushed pineapple, undrained
- 3/4 stick butter, melted
- 1 prepared pie crust

## Instructions
1. Bake prepared pie crust at 350 for 10 minutes. (Weigh the middle. I now know what pie weights are for.)
1. Beat eggs by themselves, then add melted butter and remaining ingredients. Stir to combine
1. Pour into baked pie crust, and bake at 350 for 45 mins or until the center is set.
