* Chicken breast
* Rotel tomatoes
* Scallions
* Cilantro lime dressing
* Little bit of Italian 3\-cheese dressing
* whole small bag of fancy blend cheese
* Oregano
* Minced garlic

Poked holes in the chicken breasts to let seasonings penetrate. Sprinkled with oregano, minced garlic. Covered with dressings and Rotel tomatoes.  Cooked for 1/2 hour and covered with scallions and cheese.  Put back in to let cheese melt.

We all devoured it.  Ty's chicken was half gone about 30 seconds in.

Served with:

* Spinach with feta
* Mashed potatoes
