James Bolognese's mom's eggplant recipe (from North Shore Cooks group on Facebook)  
  
I cut my eggplant length wise , egg wash , bread crumbs layered 5times I use provolone mozzarella. Each one layered with gravy/sauce provolone cheese and mozzarella baked at 350 until done 35 to 40 minutes. We prefer our at room temperature vs right out of oven.i pan fry the eggplant
