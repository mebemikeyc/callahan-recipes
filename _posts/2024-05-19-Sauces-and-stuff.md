**Sassy Sauce** 
1 1/4 cups commercial chili sauce (whole 12 oz jar)  
4 cups ketchup (whole 38 oz bottle)  
1/2 cup molasses  
1/2 cup spicy brown mustard  
1/4 cup Dijon mustard  
1/4 cup yellow mustard  
1/3 cup Worcestershire sauce  
1/4 cup cider vinegar  
1 tbsp garlic powder  
1 tbsp onion powder  
1 tbsp freshly ground black pepper  
1 tbsp salt  
1 1/2 tsp dried thyme  
1 tbsp chipotle powder  
1 tsp cayenne pepper  
1/4 tsp ground cumin  
1/2 cup honey  
1 1/2 cup dark brown sugar  
**Snappy Sauce** 
3 cups ketchup  
1/2 cup spicy brown mustard  
1/2 cup cider vinegar  
3/4 cup chili sauce  
1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce  
1 tbsp garlic powder  
1 tbsp onion powder  
2 tbsp freshly ground black pepper  
1 tbsp salt  
1 1/2 tsp thyme  
1 tbsp ground chipotle chili powder  
2 to 4 tbsp cayenne pepper  
  
**Trouble Sauce**See Snappy, but replace cayenne with habanero chili powder.  Test to get ratios right.**Torment Sauce**See Snappy, but replace cayenne with ghost chili powder. Test to get ratios right.
