Last time I used mahi mahi, but Seabrook doesn't seem to sell it.  Went with Haddock this time.  
  
Anyway, I use blackened seasoning and my mango salsa.  
  
Mango salsa:  
  
* Diced mangos
* Red onion
* Cilantro
* Lime juice
* Hot peppers \-\- Habanero is my favorite, but jalapeño is more easily tolerated

  
Blackened seasoning tends to be:* Paprika
* salt
* pepper
* onion powder
* garlic powder
* thyme
* oregano
* cayenne / ground chile powder of your choice.

  
Last time I left out the cayenne, making the heat of the salsa the star.  But, there was no heat in the salsa either.  
  
Great blackening spice.  Was amazing on pork "country style ribs"
