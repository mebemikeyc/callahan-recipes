Followed the instructions from "confessions of a foodie" site.  First time trying to make carnitas.  They came out amazing.  Will definitely make them again, but will adjust the salt levels.  
Ingredients:* 16 ounce container of lard
* 1 cup water
* Around 2 pounds of pork butt.  Cut into roughly 3 inch pieces
* 2 oranges, halved
* 4 cloves of garlic, peeled
* 2 sticks of cinnamon
* 1 medium onion, peeled and halved
* 4 bay leaves
* 2 tablespoons kosher salt
* 
* 1 tablespoon dried whole Mexican oregano, crushed between palms
* 1 teaspoon dried thyme
* 1 cup evaporated milk
* ½ cup beer (nothing too hoppy)

Toppings I used* Oaxaca cheese, shredded
* Chopped (food processed) cilantro mixed with chopped onion and lime juice
* Restaurant style salsa
* Cholula hot sauce
* Soft corn tortillas

Instructions  
Pre\-cook1. Trim rind, excess fat and cut out bones.
2. I dry\-brined the pieces in salt overnight.  Because of this, the salt added was definitely excessive.  Will reduce a lot next time.
  
Cook1. Put lard and water in cold dutch oven.  Heat on medium\-high heat until lard liquifies.
2. Add pork to pot, careful not to splash hot fat.
3. Squeeze juice from oranges into pot and add orange peels to the pot.
4. Simmer on medium, uncovered, for one hour. Stir the meat every 15 minutes to ensure it doesn't stick to the bottom of the pot.
5. Add the garlic, onion, cinnamon, bay leaves, salt, oregano, thyme, milk and beer to the pot. Stir well to combine. Continue simmering on medium heat, uncovered, for an additional hour. Stir meat at least every 20 minutes to make sure it's cooking evenly.
6. Using a slotted spoon, remove meat and let it cool on paper lined plate or tray.
  
Finishing1. Remove excess fat from cooled pork and break into smaller, fryable pieces.  (Bite size but not too small.)
2. Heat a frying pan and add some of the leftover lard.
3. Fry cooled pork until somewhat crispy but not TOO crispy.  Should still be soft inside.
