This recipe changes a lot, depending on my mood.  Everyone seems to love my guacamole.

* \~6\-8 ripe avocados, mashed
* 1/2 large red onion, food processed fine
* 1/2 to 1 bunch cilantro, roughly, depending on size. Washed and finely food processed
* 1 can Ro Tel tomatoes, drained
* Fresh squeezed lime juice to taste.  Usually use 4 limes
* Goya Adobo, salt, pepper to taste
* Sometimes I add a dash of cumin
* Sometimes I add a little minced garlic or garlic powder

Combine all the ingredients in a bowl and mash with a potato masher.
