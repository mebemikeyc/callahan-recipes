Par cook broccoli and kill cooking process by running under cold water

Fillet chicken breasts in half lengthwise.  Coat with Slap Ya Mama low sodium blend and toss in vegetable oil to coat.  Grill to 165 degrees on a meat thermometer.  Let rest and cut in bite size slices.

Cook ziti / ziti rigati to "tender" recipe.  Prince ziti was 10 minutes.  Drain and set aside.

Make alfredo sauce.  Made one batch of this recipe, but probably could have used a double recipe to make it extra saucy.

Ingredients:

* 1 stick Cup Butter
* 1 ½ Cups Heavy Cream
* 2 cloves garlic, minced in garlic press
* ½ Teaspoon Italian Seasoning
* ½ Teaspoon Salt
* ¼ Teaspoon Pepper
* 2 Cups Freshly Grated Parmesan Cheese (used whole block from Market Basket)

Instructions

* Add the butter and cream to a large skillet.
* Simmer over low heat for 2 minutes.
* Whisk in the garlic, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper for one minute.
* Whisk in the parmesan cheese until melted.
* Serve immediately.
