A simple, filling soup that tastes even better the next day. It uses pantry staples and comes together in one pot.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1½ cups dried brown or green lentils, rinsed
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 6 cups vegetable broth
- 1 bay leaf
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped, for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery and cook for 6 to 8 minutes, until softened.
2. Stir in the garlic, cumin, and paprika and cook for 1 minute, until fragrant.
3. Add the lentils, tomatoes, broth, and bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer, partially covered, for 30 to 35 minutes, until the lentils are tender.
4. Remove the bay leaf. For a thicker soup, blend about a third of it with an immersion blender, or mash lightly with a spoon.
5. Stir in the lemon juice and season with salt and pepper. Serve topped with parsley.

## Notes

- **Storage:** Keeps in the fridge for up to 5 days. The soup thickens as it sits, so thin it with a splash of water or broth when reheating.
- **Freezing:** Freezes well for up to 3 months.
- **Variations:** Add a handful of chopped spinach or kale in the last 5 minutes, or stir in a spoonful of plain yogurt when serving.
