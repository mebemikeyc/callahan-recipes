I originally made this pico de gallo for my Chipotle copycat "burrito bowls", inspired by a lunch at Chipotle with Yaya.  I loosely followed a recipe online. The proportions of the ingredients depend on the day... Measure with your heart.

When I make it for tacos, I usually leave the onions more on the chunkier side, making them more like pickled onions.  Also, I add more jalapeño, and leave that chunkier too.  In that case, I call it a "salsa cruda", though the terms are really synonymous.

Ingredients
-----------

* 1 1/2 cups Roma tomatoes chopped (cored and seeds removed)
* 1/2 cup cilantro chopped
* 2 1/2 tbsp jalapeño pepper finely diced
* 1/2 red onion finely diced
* 1/2 tbsp Kosher salt
* 2 tbsp fresh lime juice

Instructions
------------

1. Core and seed the tomatoes
2. Prep all the other ingredients
3. Combine and season to taste
