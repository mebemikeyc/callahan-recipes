I originally made this pico de gallo for my "burrito bowls".  Inspired by a lunch at Chipotle with Yaya.  I loosely followed a recipe online. The proportions of the ingredients depend on the day.  

Ingredients
-----------

* 1 1/2 cups Roma tomatoes chopped (cored and seeds removed)
* 1/2 cup cilantro chopped
* 2 1/2 tbsp jalapeno pepper finely diced
* 1/2 red onion finely diced
* 1/2 tbsp Kosher salt
* 2 tbsp fresh lime juice

Instructions
------------

1. Core and seed the tomatoes
2. Prep all the other ingredients
3. Combine and season to taste
