Followed recipe except I added crushed red pepper to the sauce, ginger and cashews to the stir fry when adding the garlic.  Also added lime juice to final mix.  Seemed a little on the sweet side and could stand to have more orange.  Maybe orange zest

Stir fry:

* 2 pounds Boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut to small pieces, seasoned with S\&P
* 2 Red pepper cut into bite size pieces
* A bunch of minced garlic
* A bunch of freshly grated ginger
* Some oil
* Cashews
* Pineapple from 1 can of pineapple chunks
* Juice of one lime

Heat up oil in pan and cook chicken till it's browned all around.  About 5 minutes.Add red pepper and pineapple and cook until softened.  About 5 minutesAdd garlic, ginger and cashewsAdd sauce and simmer for a bit to incorporate flavorsSqueeze juice of 1 lime to the mixture  
Sauce:* 1 cup pineapple juice (from 1 can after pineapple removed)
* 1/2 cup orange juice
* 1/2 cup soy sauce
* 1/2 cup low sodium chicken stock
* 2/3 cup hoisin sauce
* 1 cup brown sugar (Used molasses and white sugar.  1 cup sounds like a LOT)
* Good scoop of minced garlic
* 4 tsp corn starch
* Hefty pinch of crushed red pepper

Add all ingredients to pan and simmer till thickened
