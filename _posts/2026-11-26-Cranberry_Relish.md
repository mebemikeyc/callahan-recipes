### Ingredients
- One bag of fresh cranberries, 12 oz
- 1 orange, cut in chunks
- 1 cup white sugar

### Instructions
1. Add all ingredients to a food processor.
1. Process until mixed and smooth, periodically scraping down the dides of the bowl.
1. Transfer to a serving dish and enjoy!
