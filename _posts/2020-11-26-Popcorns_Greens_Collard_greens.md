### Ingredients

* 2 medium/large onions cut root to tip
* 4 cloves of garlic, smashed and rough chopped
* 4 strips of thick cut bacon
* 3 bunches, though small, collard greens \-\- Stems removed and cut into strips
* apple cider vinegar
* hot sauce
* Generous handful of pulled pork, rough chopped
* 2 cups chicken broth (water and bouillon)

### Instructions

1. Cut up bacon and render in the pot. When crispy, remove bacon (for now)
2. Cook onions and garlic in the rendered bacon fat
3. Add bacon and pork
4. Add apple cider vinegar, hot sauce, chicken broth
5. Add collards and cover.  Allow to boil for a bit to wilt the collards.
6. Turn down to low and simmer for about an hour
