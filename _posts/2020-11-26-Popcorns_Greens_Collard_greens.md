Every Thanksgiving, Crystal's dad, John, AKA Popcorn, Daddy John, brought the collard greens.  They were a huge hit, and became what everyone looked forward to most.  This is his recipe, as he described it.  When making these, it's important to listen to Otis Redding's "Sittin' on the Dock of the Bay", and whistle along.  Popcorn didn't have pulled pork on hand, so he used smoked turkey legs, ham hocks, or whatever smoked meat was available.  I make pulled pork in advance just to have some ready for Thanksgiving greens.

### Ingredients
- 2 medium/large onions cut root to tip
- 4 cloves of garlic, smashed and rough chopped
- 4 strips of thick cut bacon
- 3 bunches, though small, collard greens - Stems removed and cut into strips
- apple cider vinegar
- hot sauce
- Generous handful of pulled pork, rough chopped
- 2 cups chicken broth (water and bouillon)

### Instructions
1. Cut up bacon and render in the pot. When crispy, remove bacon (for now)
1. Cook onions and garlic in the rendered bacon fat
1. Add bacon and pork
1. Add apple cider vinegar, hot sauce, chicken broth
1. Add collards and cover.  Allow to boil for a bit to wilt the collards.
1. Turn down to low and simmer for about an hour
