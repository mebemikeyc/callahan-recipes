This was one of my Dad's favorite soups.  It's still one of mine.  I find myself digging up the recipe every couple months, regardless of season or weather.  So, here it is.  
**Soup**10 cups waterEnough beef bouillon for water2 teaspoons oregano4 teaspoons ground cumin1/2 bunch fine chopped cilantro2 28 oz cans of chopped tomatoesChopped veggies, 2 cups each:* Carrots
* Bell Pepper
* Onion
* Zucchini
* Summer Squash
* Celery

**Meatballs**1/2 bunch fine chopped cilantro1 cup rice2 eggs2 lbs lean ground beef  
Combine all top ingredients and simmer for awhile while making meatballs.  
Combine all the meatball ingredients, shape into small meatballs and gently drop into soup.  
Simmer for roughly 20 mins or until meatballs are fully cooked and veggies are tender.
