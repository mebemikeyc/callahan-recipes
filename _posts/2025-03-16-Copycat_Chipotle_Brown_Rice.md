This is the rice I use for my "burrito bowls". This recipe is totally ripped off from some website.  I cooked it in the rice cooker, which really kinda made a mess.  Apparently I didn't really read the recipe, as it said to put the cilantro in after cooking.

## Ingredients
- 4 cups water
- 2 cups brown rice
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1 teaspoon extra\-virgin olive oil
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- ½ cup chopped fresh cilantro

## Instructions
1. Prepare brown rice in a rice cooker, using brown rice instructions, or Bring water and brown rice to a boil in a saucepan. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer until rice is tender and liquid has been absorbed, 30 to 45 minutes.
1. Transfer cooked rice to a large serving bowl and cool slightly, 5 to 7 minutes.
1. Whisk together lime juice, garlic, olive oil, and salt in a small bowl. stir into rice. Fold in cilantro.
