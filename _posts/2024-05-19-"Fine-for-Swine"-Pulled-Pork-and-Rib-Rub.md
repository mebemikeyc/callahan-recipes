This is a fairly low salt rub that I like a lot on ribs. It's good on pulled pork too.  For pork belly though, you're gonna want less salt.

Ingredients:

* 1/2 cup brown sugar
* 1 tablespoon kosher salt
* 1 1/2 tablespoon black pepper
* 1 tablespoon smoked paprika or chipotle powder
* 1 tablespoon garlic powder / granulated garlic
* 1 tablespoon onion powder / granulated onion
* 1 tablespoon ground mustard
* 1/2 tablespoon celery salt
* 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
* 3/8 teaspoon ground allspice
