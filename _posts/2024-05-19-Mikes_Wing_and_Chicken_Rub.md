This is based on Ray Lampe's "Big Time BBQ Rub".  I omit the nutmeg, and usually substitute the cayenne for chipotle powder.  Also, when I first started, I thought "granulated" meant "minced", when it's actually closer to powder.  Well, I got used to it with big pieces of garlic and onion, so now I gotta keep with it, and use half and half.

This has a pretty distinctive taste.  It's amazing on wings and chicken.  It can easily be a little salty, so careful not to use too much.

Ingredients:

* 1/2 cup salt
* 1/2 cup turbinado sugar
* 1/4 cup firmly packed brown sugar
* 2 tablespoons paprika
* 2 tablespoons chili powder
* 2 tablespoons freshly ground pepper
* 1/2 tablespoon granulated garlic / garlic powder
* 1/2 tablespoon minced garlic
* 1/2 tablespoon granulated onion / onion powder
* 1/2 tablespoon minced onion
* 1 tablespoon dried thyme
* 1 tablespoon ground cumin
* 2 teaspoons cayenne pepper (usually substitute chipotle powder)
