Thrown together at the last minute and came out great.  Here's the stuff I added.  Ratios are "till it looks good".  Crystal added more fresh basil and feta.

* 1 lb Orzo
* olive oil
* red wine vinegar
* lots of fresh basil
* 1/2 red onion
* chopped orange pepper (other color peppers looked like shit)
* 1/2 peeled, cubed english cucumber
* bunch of cubed feta
* little dash of oregano
* multi colored cherry tomatoes, quartered
