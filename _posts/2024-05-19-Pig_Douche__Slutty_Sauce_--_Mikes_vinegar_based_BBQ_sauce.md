This is pretty darn simple.  It's tasty on pulled pork.  Gives it kind of a Carolina style taste.

Ingredients

* 2 cups cider vinegar
* 1/4 cup brown sugar
* 1 tablespoon salt
* 1 tablespoon very finely ground black pepper
* 1/2 teaspoon cayenne
