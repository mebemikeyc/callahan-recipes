This is the recipe I use for tortillas.  I weighed out the balls to 34 grams each.  That made 13 balls.  Might be better a little smaller.

### 

### INGREDIENTS

* 1 ½cupsmasa harina
* ¼teaspoonsalt
* 2tablespoonsvegetable oil, lard or butter
* About 1 cuphot water, or more as needed
* Flour for kneading

  

### PREPARATION

Trying out a different ratio:2 cups masa harina1/2 tsp salt2 tbsp vegetable oil1 1/2 to 2 cups of hot water  
Was WAY too watery. Did 2 cups water.  Needed to add a bunch more masa. As a result, I had to add more salt.
