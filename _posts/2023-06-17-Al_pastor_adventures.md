Pretty good base recipe: https://heygrillhey.com/smoked\-al\-pastor/  
Last time I smoked a pork shoulder picnic, I cut big grooves in the meat, holding on by the bone.  Marinated for a day then put it on the smoker.  Might have overcooked, but it was delicious.  
  
  
Followed that marinade recipe again, but used ancho powder instead of ancho chiles.  Used a whole bottle and then some, since I couldn't find a conversion.  The Mexican oregano makes all the difference in the world  
  
Base recipe2 Tablespoons butter¼ cup white onion (diced)o4 cloves garlic½ cup chicken stock2 dried ancho chili peppers2 chipotle chili peppers in adobo sauce1 ½ Tablespoons achiote paste (or powder)2 teaspoons cumin2 teaspoons Mexican oregano1 ½ Tablespoons kosher salt1 Tablespoon sugar⅓ cup white vinegar  
Combo I used1 onion, diced \-\- 1 1/4 cup4 tbsp butter8 tbsp garlic1 cup chicken broth4 chipotles2\.25 oz ancho chile powder3 tbsp achiote seeds, ground4 tsp cumin4 tsp mexican oregano3 tbsp kosher salt2 tbsp sugar2/3 cup white vinegar  
  
  
Combo I used with chiles available1 onion, diced4 tbsp butter8 cloves garlic1 cup chicken stock4 chipotles4 ancho chile peppers3 tbsp achiote seeds, ground4 tsp cumin4 tsp mexican oregano3 tbsp kosher salt2 tbsp sugar2/3 cup white vinegar  
Cook onions and garlic in butter until soft.  About 2\-3 mins  
Add chicken stock, chopotles and anchos.  Cook for 4\-5 mins  
Add achiote, cumin, mexican oregano, salt, sugar and vinegar.  Cook another 4\-5 mins
