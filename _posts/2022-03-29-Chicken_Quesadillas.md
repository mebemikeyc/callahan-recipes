The wife loves the Taco Bell chicken quesadillas.  The sauce makes them what they are.  There's lots of nearly identical copycat recipes online for the sauce.  This is the combination I used that we liked.  The chicken, I just halved breasts lengthwise and marinated in taco seasoning and oil to make it stick and add some fat.  Then grilled and diced.

### Copycat Quesadilla Sauce

* 1/2 cup sour cream
* 1/2 cup mayonnaise
* 8 tsp pickled jalapeños, minced
* 8 tsp pickled jalapeño juice
* 2 2/3 teaspoons sugar
* 2 teaspoons cumin
* 2 teaspoons paprika
* 1/2 teaspoon cayenne
* 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder

Mix all ingredients together and put in a squeeze bottle for easy distribution.  

### Quesadilla Assembly

Heat a frying pan with a little bit of oil.Put a tortilla in the pan.  Top the whole thing with cheese, put diced chicken and quesadilla sauce on one side.  When cheese starts melting, fold it over with a spatula.  Flip occasionally until it reaches desired toastiness and meltiness.
