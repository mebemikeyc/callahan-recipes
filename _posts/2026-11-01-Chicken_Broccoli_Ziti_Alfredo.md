I first made this while Crystal was on vacation.  I probably made it more work than it had to be, but everyone liked it.  I've made it a few times since then, and it's always a hit.

## Ingredients
- 2 large chicken breasts, ~2 pounds
- 14 ounces fresh broccoli florets
- Slap Ya Mama cajun seasoning
- Potato starch
- 2 eggs, beaten and mixed with milk for egg wash
- Mixture of masa harina and all purpose flour for coating.  Roughly 70/30 ratio of AP flour to corn.
- Oil for frying. Corn, vegetable, canola, something like that.
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese (optional)

### Alfredo Ingredients
- 1 stick of unsalted butter
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 2 cloves of garlic, pressed
- 1/2 teaspoon italian seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 2 cups (200 grams) freshly grated parmesan cheese

## Instructions

### Chicken
1. Cut the chicken into bite size pieces
1. In a large bowl, toss the chicken with Slap Ya Mama Cajun seasoning and potato starch to coat
1. Egg wash and coat with flour/masa mix.  Repeat until all coated.
1. Fry at 350º in batches and set aside to dry.

### Broccoli
1. Cut broccoli into bite size pieces
1. Blanch in boiling water until starting to get tender, but not too tender
1. Chill in cold water until sufficiently cooled, then drain and set aside.

### Alfredo Sauce
1. Melt the butter in a sauce pan
1. Add the garlic and cook until it softens up a little.  Don't want it too raw
1. Add the remaining seasonings and simmer for bit
1. Add the cream and bring to a simmer.
1. Add the cheese and stir until it melts.
1. Simmer for 1-2 minutes until it thickens.

### Assembly
1. Cook the pasta to the box instructions.
1. When the pasta is almost done, add the broccoli back in for everything to finish together.
1. Drain and put in a large bowl.  Toss with the alfredo sauce and fried chicken.
1. If using, mix in the shredded mozzarella cheese for an extra cheese pull
