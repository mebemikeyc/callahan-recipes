### Ingredients

#### Stock
This recipe is a double of the original because I include the spine too.

- 1 neck, giblets and liver from a turkey
- Spine and wing tips from the turkey
- 12 cups of water
- 2 onions, quartered
- 2 stalks of celery, sliced
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 2 teaspoons of whole black peppercorns
- 2 bay leaves

#### Gravy
- 1 stick of butter
- 1/2 cup of flour
- 1 quart of turkey stock, made above


### Instructions

#### Stock
1. Add everything but the turkey liver to a stock pot and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer for about an hour, skimming occasionally.
1. Add the liver and simmer for another 30 minutes.

#### Gravy
1. Make a roux with the butter and flour.
1. Gradually add the stock to the roux and stir to absorb.
1. Season as needed with salt and pepper.
1. Add more liquid if it gets too thick.
