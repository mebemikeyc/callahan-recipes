### Ingredients
- 1 orange, zested and juiced
- 1 bag of fresh cranberries, 12 oz, reserving 1/2 cup
- enough water to make 1 cup liquid after orange juice (I needed ~1/2 cup)
- 3/4 cup light brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

### Instructions
1. Zest then juice the orange.  It's easier to zest a whole orange. Set the zest aside.
1. Add water to orange juice to make 1 cup of liquid.
1. Put cranberries (1/2 cup removed for later), orange juice liquid and sugar in a sauce pan and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer until berries have popped and it has thickened.  Roughly 15 minutes.
1. Take off the heat and add orange zest, vanilla extract and 1/2 cup reserved cranberries.  Let cool before transferring to a serving dish.
