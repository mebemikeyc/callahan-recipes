### Ingredients

* 2 cups long grain rice. I used jasmine
* 1/8 cup vegetable oil
* 1/2 onion chopped
* 1 poblano rough chopped
* 4 or 5 cloves of garlic, chopped
* 8 oz can of Goya tomato sauce. Used low sodium
* 1 tsp salt
* 1 tsp cumin
* 1 tsp black pepper
* 1 heaping tablespoon chopped cilantro
* 4 cups boiling water
* 4 cubes of Knorr caldo de tomate con pollo

### Instructions

1. Add oil and rice to a pot.  Heat to medium high and fry until golden brown.
1. Prep broth, with Knorr caldo de tomate and boiling water, while rice is toasting.
1. Add onion, garlic and poblano.  Stir in and cook a few minutes more.
1. When more browned, add broth, tomato sauce, spices and cilantro.
1. Stir, cover and simmer 30\-40 mins until water is absorbed.
1. Pull off heat and let sit for 15 mins or so.  Fluff and serve.
