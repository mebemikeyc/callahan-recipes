These are the beans I use for my "burrito bowls".  Again, totally ripped off from a website.

Ingredients
-----------

* 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
* 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
* 16 ounces dried pinto beans, rinsed
* 1 teaspoon ground cumin
* 1 teaspoon dried oregano
* 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
* 2 bay leaves
* 1 (7 1/16 ounce) can chipotle chiles in adobo
* 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice, from one lemon
* 2 tablespoons lime juice, from one lime
* 2 tablespoons kosher salt
* 1 teaspoon fresh ground black pepper

Instructions
------------

1. Heat oil in a large heavy\-bottomed pot, or dutch oven medium\-high.
2. Add onion, beans, cumin, oregano, garlic, bay leaf, 2 chipotle chilies, and 2 teaspoons adobo sauce from the same can. Add enough water to pot to cover beans.
3. Bring to a simmer and cook until beans are tender, but not falling apart. Remove from heat. Remove bay leaves and chipotle chilies. Stir in lemon juice, lime juice, salt and pepper.
