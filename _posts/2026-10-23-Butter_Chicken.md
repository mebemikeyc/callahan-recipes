In early 2020, I started making a chicken curry that my sons really loved. It was very much NOT an Indian curry, but it was enjoyable.  My results were inconsistent, but I didn't really have a recipe. Kinda just added curry powder and other ingredients until I liked it.  I followed a recipe [here](https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/butter-chicken/) but found it really hard to follow.


## Ingredients

### For the Marinade

- 1/2 KG boneless chicken. Cut into 1" pieces if frying, if grilling, keep big.
- 3/4 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1/3 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1/2 tablespoon grated garlic
- 1/2 tablespoon fine minced garlic (or pressed)
- 1/8 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin powder
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder
- 1 teaspoon kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

### For the Sauce

- 2 - 3 tablespoons of butter
- 2 inch piece of cinnamon stick
- 2 - 4 green cardamom pods
- 2 - 4 cloves
- 1 1/2 (130 grams) sliced onions
- 1 cup crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 tablespoon grated ginger
- 1/2 tablespoon fine minced garlic (or pressed)
- 1 - 2 green chiles. (I added 2 jalapeños for a triple batch and it was a lot. I liked it, but it was almost too spicy for Crystal)
- 42 grams whole cashews, soaked in hot water
- 2 teaspoons Kashmiri chili powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
- 1 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin

### Final Assembly

- 3/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoon kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
- 1 cup hot water (to thin sauce)
- 1/3 cup heavy cream
- 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro leaves


## Instructions


### Chicken Marinade
The original instructions suggest doing separate marinade steps.  Start with salt, lemon juice and Kashmiri chili powder, then another with all the other ingredients.  I'm applying my own strategy here that I'll use next time.

1. Mix dry ingredients with lemon juice and oil.  Mix until thoroughly combined.
1. Mix in ginger and garlic.  Mix again.
1. Add Greek yogurt and mix completely.
1. Add to the chicken, making sure it's evenly coated.

### Masala Sauce

#### Tomato / Onion / Cashew Cream

1. Fry the onions in oil until golden. Take off the heat and allow to cool.
1. Add the cashews to a blender with just enough liquid to blend and form a cream.
1. Add the onions and blend thoroughly.
1. Add the crushed tomatoes and blend until smooth.


#### Sauce

1. Melt butter and fry whole spices until they begin to sizzle -- Cinnamon, cloves and cardamom pods
1. Add ginger, garlic and chilis (if using) and fry on low heat until fragrant but not burning.  A few minutes.
1. Take off the heat and add in the spices (chili powder, garam masala, coriander and cumin)
1. Add the tomato / cashew puree.  Bring to a boil then reduce heat and simmer until it begins to thicken.
1. Add water and continue to simmer for about 10 minutes.  If you can find them, discard the whole spices at this point. (I could only find the cinnamon)

#### Assembly

1. Grill the chicken and dice to 1 inch pieces.  Alternatively, pan fry the 1 inch pieces, if making that way.  (Can be done before starting the sauce too.)
1. Add the chicken to the sauce and mix in to cover. Simmer for 5 - 10 minutes until it's nice and tender.  If too thick, add water to thin it out.
1. Stir in salt, sugar and kasuri methi.  Taste test and add more garam masala or butter as needed.
1. Turn off heat and stir in heavy cream. Garnish with chopped cilantro leaves.  Serve with basmati rice.
