I first made these to go with a copycat of Olive Garden's Zuppa Toscana.  Breadsticks go pretty well with their soup, but their food has gotten pretty gross.  I was impressed how good these came out.

### Ingredients
- 1 1/4 cups warm water (100-110ªF)
- 1 packet of active dry yeast, or 2 1/4 teaspoons (7g)
- 2 tbsp granulated sugar
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 3 1/3 cups (433 grams) bread flour
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder (optional)

### Topping
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter, nelted
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder

### Instructions
1. Whisk the warm water, yeast, and sugar together in the bowl of your stand mixer. Let it sit for 5–10 minutes until foamy and frothy on top.
1. Add the melted butter, salt, 1 cup (130g) bread flour, and garlic powder, if using. With a dough hook attachment, mix on low speed for 30 seconds, scrape down the sides of the bowl with a silicone spatula, then add the remaining flour. Beat on medium speed until the dough comes together and pulls away from the sides of the bowl, about 2 minutes.
1. Keep the dough in the mixer and beat for an additional 8-10 full minutes.
1. Transfer the dough to a greased bowl, cover and allow to rest to rise in a warm place for 1 1/2 - 2 hours.  Dough should double in size.
1. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
1. Divide dough to equal parts and roll out to breadstick shape, roughly 8 inches long.  Place on the parchment-lined baking sheets.
1. Allow the breadsticks to rise a second time for about 20 mins.  I was supposed to cover them, but I didn't. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 400ºF.
1. Bake for 18-20 minutes, rotating half way through.
1. Melt the butter and combine with salt and garlic powder for the topping.
1. Let the breadsticks cool for a couple minutes, then brush on the melted butter.
