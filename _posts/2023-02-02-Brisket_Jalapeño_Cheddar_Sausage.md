I got a meat grinder / sausage stuffer for Christmas!!  These are the first sausages I tried making.  It's based on this recipe, but instead of pork and beef blend, I used brisket fat and brisket meat: https://twoguysandacooler.com/jalapeno\-cheddar\-sausage/

**Ingredients**

* 700 grams lean meat
* 300 grams fat
* 16 grams Kosher salt
* 2\.5 grams curing salt \#1
* 3 grams black pepper
* 5 grams garlic powder
* 1\.5 grams onion powder
* 0\.5 grams sugar
* 2\.5 grams chipotle
* 2\.5 grams paprika
* 6 grams mustard seed
* 2\.5 grams red pepper flakes
* 20 grams nonfat milk powder
* 60 ml cream
* 30 grams jalapeños
* 100 grams cubed sharp cheddar

I followed the instructions in the recipe, but I think everything should have been colder.  I did a double batch, which was too big for the mixing bowl to mix the ingredients without making a big mess.  And, it really didn't mix as well as I had hoped.  Next time I will probably start mixing by hand first, then machine mixing in smaller batches.  
Also, I don't think I soaked the casings long enough.  They tore really easy.  
Then, I smoked them until they were done.  Everyone thought they were the best sausages ever.
