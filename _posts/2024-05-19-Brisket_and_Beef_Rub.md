I like this one on brisket and beef in general.  If I'm not just doing salt, pepper, garlic and a little Lowry's seasoning salt, this is what I'm using.  Not much salt or sugar, not overboard on flavors, decent kick.

Ingredients

* 3/4 cup paprika
* 1/4 cup coarse kosher salt
* 1/4 cup white sugar
* 1/4 cup freshly ground black pepper
* 1/4 cup chili powder
* 2 tbsp garlic powder
* 2 tbsp dried minced garlic
* 2 tbsp onion powder
* 2 tbsp dried minced onion
* 1 tbsp cayenne
