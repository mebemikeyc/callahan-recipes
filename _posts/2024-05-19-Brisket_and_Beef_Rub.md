I like this one on brisket and beef in general.  If I'm not just doing SPOG (salt, pepper, onion, garlic), this is what I'm using.  Not much salt or sugar, not overboard on flavors, decent kick.  It IS heavy on paprika, which is arguably just for color.  I think it gives it a little bark sweetness too.

## Ingredients
- 3/4 cup paprika
- 1/4 cup coarse kosher salt
- 1/4 cup white sugar
- 1/4 cup freshly ground black pepper
- 1/4 cup chili powder
- 2 tbsp garlic powder
- 2 tbsp dried minced garlic
- 2 tbsp onion powder
- 2 tbsp dried minced onion
- 1 tbsp cayenne
