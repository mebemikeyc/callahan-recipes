This is the corn salsa I originally made for my "burrito bowls", which was my first time making it.  It was quite a hit.

Ingredients
-----------

* 24 ounces frozen corn (I used both yellow and sweet white corn, 1 lb bags.  Ends up being 32 oz)
* 1/2 cup red onion finely chopped
* 1 large roasted poblano pepper peeled, seeded, and finely chopped
* 2 jalapeño peppers stemmed, seeded, and finely chopped
* 1/4 cup fresh cilantro finely chopped
* 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
* 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
* Salt

Instructions
------------

1. Cook corn according to package directions. Spread on a baking sheet and put in fridge to cool.
2. To a large bowl, add cooled corn, red onion, jalapeño peppers, poblano pepper, cilantro, lemon juice, and lime juice. Season to taste with salt and serve with tortilla chips, burrito bowls, and tacos.
