These pretzel bites are always a big hit.  I follow Alton Brown's soft pretzel recipe, but cut them into bite size pieces instead of making full size pretzels.  And brush with unsalted butter at the end for extra yumminess.

Link to the original recipe: https://altonbrown.com/recipes/homemade\-soft\-pretzels/

### Ingredients

* 1 1/2 cups warm water, 110\-115ºF, plus 10 cups water for boiling
* 1 tablespoon sugar
* 2 teaspoons kosher salt
* 1 package active dry yeast
* 22 ounces all\-purpose flour
* 2 ounces (half stick) unsalted butter (melted)
* 2/3 cup baking soda
* 1 large egg yolk, beaten with 1 tablespoon water
* Vegetable oil, for the bowl and pan
* Pretzel salt

### Instructions

1. Combine the 1 1/2 cups warm water, the sugar, and kosher salt in the bowl of a stand mixer and sprinkle the yeast on top. Set aside until the mixture foams, about 5 minutes.
2. Add the flour and butter and, using the dough hook attachment, mix on low speed until well combined. Change to medium speed and knead until the dough is smooth and pulls away from the side of the bowl, 4 to 5 minutes.
3. Remove the dough from the bowl, clean the bowl, then oil it well. Return the dough to the bowl, cover with plastic wrap, and set aside in a warm place until the dough has doubled in size, 50 to 55 minutes.
4. Heat oven to 450ºF. Line 2 half\-sheet pans with parchment paper and set aside.
5. Bring the remaining 10 cups water and the baking soda to a rolling boil in a deep pot.
6. Meanwhile, turn the dough out onto a lightly floured work surface.  Roll the dough into ropes, about 1 inch in diameter.  Cut the ropes into "bite size" pieces, about 1 inch long.
7. Working with a large handful at a time, place the pretzel bites in the boiling water for 30 seconds. Remove them from the water using a slotted spoon. Put them on the sheet pans, spacing about 1 1/2 inches apart.
8. Brush the top of each pretzel bite with the beaten egg yolk and water mixture, and sprinkle with pretzel salt.
9. Bake until dark golden brown in color, 12 to 14 minutes.
10. Brush with melted unsalted butter.
